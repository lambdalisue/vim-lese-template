INTRODUCTION					*lese-zorvex-introduction*

lese-zorvex adds modules and pickers to lese. Loading it declares nothing;
bring its pickers in from your custom.lua (|lese-zorvex-usage|).

=============================================================================
REQUIREMENTS					*lese-zorvex-requirements*

lese (https://github.com/lambdalisue/vim-lese) on Neovim, or on Vim built
with |+lua|.

=============================================================================
USAGE						*lese-zorvex-usage*

Call |lese-zorvex.define_pickers()| from the function your custom.lua
returns (:LeseCustom opens it):
>lua
	return function(x)
	  -- ... your own pickers ...
	  require("lese-zorvex").define_pickers(x)
	end
<
Or use the modules in pickers of your own (|lese-zorvex-modules|).

=============================================================================
INTERFACE					*lese-zorvex-interface*

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
PICKERS						*lese-zorvex-pickers*

					*lese-zorvex.define_pickers()*
require("lese-zorvex").define_pickers({x})
	Declare the pickers below on the entrypoint {x} custom.lua receives.

						*lese-zorvex-picker-zorvex-word*
:Lese zorvex-word [{file}]
	The words of the current buffer, or of {file}, each once.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULES						*lese-zorvex-modules*

					*lese-zorvex.source.word*
require("lese-zorvex.source.word")([{opts}])
	A source that emits each word (a run of ASCII letters, digits and
	underscores) of the buffer or file in its first argument, or of the
	current buffer, once, in the order the words first occur. The detail
	holds `bufnr`, and the `line` and `column` where the word first occurs.

	{opts} may hold:
	  min_length	Skip shorter words. Default: 1
	  chunk_size	Lines read at once. Default: 1000
